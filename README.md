# Snowy

Having a delicate personal website was my biggest dream when I opened the book _HTML+CSS: from Zero to Hero_ and wrote my first webpage. It was during spring break in 2014 that I sent my crush a link to a webpage I had spent a whole week working on. Unfortunately, my crush couldn't open the website on her browser. "It works on my end, though," I typed perplexedly and ignorantly. The scenario was etched in my mind, and I eventually found the answer in another book three years later: the link I sent her started with `files://`.

I later got my first personal cloud server on Alibaba Cloud in 2018 and deployed my first application, `supervisor`, which enabled me to publish the tasks I had completed and let others take a look at them. Having gained extensive knowledge about Computer Science after moving to the United States, I decided to build a personal website once again.

I often joke that I'm a "tropical resident" because I grew up in Guangzhou, a city where people wear T-shirts in winter. Upon experiencing my first big snowfall in my life in Massachusetts, I realized I didn't like snowy days when I did shoveling. In January 2026, a big snowstorm swept America, and the snow piled up to 50 centimeters (20 inches) high. Lying in my cozy bed, I use "Snowy" as the name of this project.

This project contains the configuration files and documentation for my personal website. Unlike tedious AI-generated technical documents, everything here is written by me. It includes thoughts I want to share with friends and anyone who happens to discover this repository.

People like me, who are nerdy, geeky, and introverted, may all desire to have a personal website to showcase their rich yet fragile inner world. I hope this repository can be helpful to strangers who happen to stumble upon it.
